The quick answer

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet? It is a product reference list for organizing shopping finds, source links, prices and sizing notes. Use it to compare options; an entry alone does not verify quality, authenticity or current availability.

02 / Your reference structure

Use these columns in your own shopping sheet. Keep observations separate from information supplied by a seller.

Suggested spreadsheet fields and checking rules
Field What to record What to check
Product & source Descriptive name, original listing URL and category. Does the link still open the intended item?
Variant Color, size, model and selected options. Do the photos and price match that exact variant?
Measurements Garment dimensions or the shoe size chart, with units. How do they compare with something that fits?
Price & currency Current item price, currency and date checked. Are delivery, service charges and taxes separate?
Quality notes Available close-ups, construction details and uncertainties. Are observations based on the item or only promotional photos?
Seller terms Return conditions, stock status and stated dispatch information. Which terms apply to this product and destination?

This is a planning framework, not a downloadable inventory or a claim that listed products have been inspected.

03 / Fit comes first

Build an Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet around fit.

“Medium” is a label. Measurements give you something concrete to compare. Keep a few reference dimensions from clothing you already wear.

  • T-shirts & hoodies: chest width, shoulder width, body length and sleeve length.
  • Jackets: the same measurements, plus enough room for your intended layers.
  • Pants & shorts: waist, rise, inseam and leg opening.
  • Headwear: circumference, adjustability and the stated size range.

05 / Before checkout

Give your Oopbuy shopping list a real budget.

The item price is only one part of the calculation. Keep separate entries for domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties.

Label each amount as confirmed, estimated or unknown. Keep the original currency beside the amount, and date any conversion you make.

Planning formula: item price + domestic delivery + service charges + international shipping + applicable taxes and duties. Include a charge only once if it is already bundled into a confirmed quote.

06 / Clear answers

you save a find.

Use this Oopbuy spreadsheet guide as a research checklist. Confirm current transaction details with the relevant seller or service.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product reference list used to organize shopping finds, source links, prices, sizing notes and comparison details. A listing is a starting point for research, not proof of quality, authenticity or availability.

How do I use an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

Choose a category, open the source listing and record the exact product variant, measurements and current price. Check the seller’s terms and available quality information before estimating your total cost.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping and taxes?

Do not assume it does. Record the item price separately from domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Confirm each charge with the relevant provider.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

For clothing, compare the listing’s measurements with a garment that fits you. For shoes, compare foot length and the model-specific size chart. A familiar size label alone is not enough.

Do brand names and product photos prove authenticity?

No. A spreadsheet entry, brand name or photograph does not establish authenticity. Check the seller’s identity, provenance, purchase documentation and return terms independently.

No. This is an independent shopping reference linking to CAFANS categories. It is not operated by or endorsed by Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.
